Why Carmen

Other apps do one job.

Most tools handle a single piece. Carmen is all of them working together.

Blockers

Cold Turkey, Freedom

They block sites. They don’t know your tasks, goals or score.

Timers

Forest, Pomofocus

They count your focus time. They can’t stop you leaving.

Trackers

Toggl, RescueTime

They show where the time went, after it’s already gone.

To-do apps

Todoist, Notion

They plan your day. They can’t enforce it.
