Two ways to say no

Choose the lock for each task. Both work on the apps you list, and both log what you tried.

Hard lockA blocked app is minimized the moment it opens. You see a short message and land back on your work. No warning, no negotiating.
Soft lockA warning covers the blocked app and a countdown runs. You can continue afterwards, but the attempt is recorded as a distraction.
Block listPick the apps to block from the ones installed on your PC, or add any by name.
A counter you can seeThe number of attempts you blocked this session stays on screen, so the lock is never invisible.

Every task has its own rules

Studying, writing and practice shouldn’t share one blocklist. Each task carries its own settings.

A different time each dayRepeat every day, on specific weekdays with minutes for each, or as a total per week, two weeks or month.
Soft or hard, per taskBe strict about studying and relaxed about reading.
Per-profile browser blockingBlock one Chrome profile while another stays free, instead of an all-or-nothing browser ban.
Allowed sites per taskList only the sites a task needs, such as Wikipedia and Docs.
Vacation hoursBank spare minutes and spend them on a rough day. They count as if you had worked them.

Real screens: the task editor, per-day minutes, and the vacation dialog.

Focus in blocks. Rest on purpose.

Run a task as a Pomodoro cycle: a focus block, a break, repeat.

The lock follows the cycleBlocks are on during focus and lift during breaks, so a break is a real break.
A small timer windowIt shows the cycle, such as Focus 1/4 or Break 1/4, and the time left, and stays out of your way.

Other apps do one job

Most tools handle a single piece. Carmen puts them in one place.

Blockers

Cold Turkey, Freedom

They block sites. They don’t know your tasks, goals or score.

Timers

Forest, Pomofocus

They count your focus time. They can’t stop you leaving.

Trackers

Toggl, RescueTime

They show where the time went, after it’s already gone.

To-do apps

Todoist, Notion

They plan your day. They can’t enforce it.
