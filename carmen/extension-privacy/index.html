Carmen Focus Extension Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What the extension does

Carmen Focus blocks distracting sites during a focus session you start. It supports soft lock, hard lock, and "only these websites" lists. It also keeps a log of slips and a per-site screen-time total. It is available for Chrome and Firefox.

Data it handles

  • Websites you visit: the domain of your open tabs. Used only to decide whether a site is allowed during a session and to count time per site for the screen-time view.
  • Session state: your active task, allowed-site lists, extra sites added during a session, and slips.

Nothing else. No page content, no form data, no passwords, no search queries, no browsing history beyond the current session's tabs, no personal identifiers, and no analytics.

Where it stays

  • All of this is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your own computer.
  • The extension's only network connection is to the Carmen Focus desktop app running on the same computer, at 127.0.0.1 (localhost). That traffic never leaves your machine.
  • The extension itself sends nothing to any server on the internet, to us, or to any third party.

If you also use the Carmen Focus desktop app, that app can sync tasks and finished sessions to your own account. That is separate from the extension and covered by the desktop app's privacy policy, not this one.

No selling or sharing

No selling, no sharing, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no remote code.

Data is not used or transferred for determining creditworthiness or for lending purposes, or for any purpose unrelated to the single purpose of blocking sites during focus sessions.

Why each permission is needed

  • tabs and tabGroups: to see which site a tab is on and to close, redirect or group blocked tabs.
  • Host access to all websites: to check the address of any tab against your allowed list and to show the block page. The extension does not read or change page content for any other purpose.
  • storage: to keep session state and screen-time totals locally.
  • scripting: to show the block screen on a page.
  • alarms and notifications: to end timers on time and to tell you a session ended.
  • Access to 127.0.0.1:5847: to talk to the desktop app on the same computer.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you clear it. Removing the extension deletes its local data.

Children

There is no minimum age to use Carmen Focus. The extension collects no personal information, and all of its data stays on your own computer.
